## 1. Bottom Cap Screws
The bottom cap has 5 round flanges that attach to the base body. Are these M5 machine screws threading into screw bosses welded on the base body? Or do you need captive nuts / weld nuts on the base body side since there's no easy access from inside?

**Answer:** Phillips flat head bolts through both pieces, nylon lock nut on inside. 5x M5 bolts per cap. Countersunk holes in bottom cap for flush finish. Flange positions calculated dynamically: 3 centered between the 4 slots (42°, 112°, 183°), 2 centered between outer slots and hinge (259°, 326°). Hinge is at 292°. ALL FLANGES ARE ROUND.
