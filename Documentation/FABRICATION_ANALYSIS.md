## Cost-Saving Opportunities (to analyze one by one)

### 1. Buy pre-perforated sheet for meshes
- **Savings:** $100-300/unit
- **Current:** Laser cutting hundreds of individual 4mm holes
- **Proposed:** Buy stock 304 SS perforated sheet (4mm holes, 6mm staggered centers -- standard catalog item). Only laser cut the outer profile and slot cutouts
- **Impact:** None -- standard perf patterns match our spec
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

### 2. Split Top Cap into 2 simple bolt-together pieces
- **Savings:** $50-200/unit
- **Current:** Compound-curved single piece that follows ceramic slot contours + 3 rolled lip strips
- **Proposed:** Piece A = flat annular ring (laser cut), Piece B = inner collar (short cylinder). Bolt together with screws
- **Impact:** Thermal seal comes from ceramic disks not metal cap, so no functional loss
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

### 3. Integrate screw flanges into lip flat pattern (bend tabs, no welding)
- **Savings:** $30-80/unit
- **Current:** 10 individual round flanges welded at precise angles (5 per cap)
- **Proposed:** Cut flanges as extensions of the lip strip, fold 90 degrees. Zero welding
- **Impact:** None -- M4 loads are minimal
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

### 4. Eliminate lid retaining wall (3.3mm rolled strip)
- **Savings:** $10-20/unit
- **Current:** 3.3mm tall strip rolled to 92.5mm diameter -- nearly impossible to roll cleanly
- **Proposed:** Stamped ring from thicker stock, or skip entirely (ceramic lip self-centers)
- **Impact:** Minimal -- gravity + lip do the work
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

### 5. Reduce standoff fins from 12 to 6
- **Savings:** $10-20/unit
- **Current:** ~12 fins individually positioned and welded
- **Proposed:** 4-6 fins with snap/press-fit clips instead of welding
- **Impact:** Mesh is rigid enough with fewer supports
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

### 6. Spin-form cap lips instead of roll + weld
- **Savings:** $20-50/unit
- **Current:** Rolled lip ring + seam weld + circumferential fillet weld per cap
- **Proposed:** Spin-form lip directly from disk edge on lathe -- one piece, no weld
- **Impact:** Better -- stronger, cleaner look
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

### 7. Use tube stock for inner housing
- **Savings:** $20-40/unit
- **Current:** Rolled from flat sheet + seam weld
- **Proposed:** Buy 304 SS welded tube near 141.7mm ID, cut to length, add holes/slots
- **Impact:** None -- housing is not structural
- **Status:** [ ] Not started

---

## Design Principles Going Forward

- **SCREWS over welding** wherever possible -- easier to assemble, disassemble, and repair
- **Buy stock materials** (pre-perforated sheet, tube stock) instead of fabricating from scratch
- **Split complex parts** into simpler bolt-together pieces
- **Minimize custom forming** -- prefer laser cut + brake bend over compound curves
