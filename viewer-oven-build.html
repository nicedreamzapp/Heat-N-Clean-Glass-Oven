Old way vs new way · 旧方法和新方法

Bottom row ×5. Before: the nuts sat inside 01 and were sealed under 03 for good. Now: welded inside 01, you bolt from outside, and it comes apart later.
底圈 ×5。以前：螺母在 01 里面，被 03 永久封在下面。现在：焊在 01 里面，从外面拧螺栓，以后能拆。

Seat row ×5. Before: you held the nuts behind the 03 wall from inside the tube. Now: welded to the 03 wall, bolts from outside.
座圈 ×5。以前：要从管子里面扶住 03 墙后面的螺母。现在：焊在 03 墙上，螺栓从外面装。

Top row ×5. Before: impossible, the nuts were buried behind the core and 14.6 mm of insulation. Now: welded inside the top of 01, so core, insulation and cap go in first and the bolts go in last.
顶圈 ×5。以前：根本装不了，螺母埋在加热芯和 14.6 mm 保温棉后面。现在：焊在 01 顶部里面，先装加热芯、保温棉和顶盖，最后装螺栓。

Lower lid row ×5. Before: you held the nuts behind the 09 wall. Now: welded to 09.
盖子下圈 ×5。以前：要扶住 09 墙后面的螺母。现在：焊在 09 上。

Upper lid row ×5. Before: you held the nuts inside 06. Now: welded inside 06.
盖子上圈 ×5。以前：要在 06 里面扶住螺母。现在：焊在 06 里面。

Hinge. Before: on a lid bolt at 257.5°. Now: centred at 292.5° in the slot-free arc, on its own 2 holes per leaf, with weld nuts.
合页。以前：装在 257.5° 的盖子螺栓上。现在：中心在没有玻璃槽那段弧的 292.5°，每片有自己的 2 个孔和焊接螺母。

The shop welds all 29 nuts at the factory before shipping. Assembly is about 5-6 min vs 45-60+ (an estimate), and the oven can be taken apart for service.
工厂在出厂前把 29 颗螺母全部焊好。装配大约 5-6 分钟，以前要 45-60 分钟以上（估计），而且炉子可以拆开维修。
